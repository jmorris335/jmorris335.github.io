About Me

By label I'm a mechanical engineer, which means I'm a generalist with a few key interests in topics such as computer science, systems engineering, engineering design, dynamics, and applied category theory. My favorite roles are as husband and dad.

I recently obtained my PhD in mechanical engineering from Clemson University under Dr.s John Wagner and Gregory Mocko. My research is focused on establishing unifying modeling structures that can be used to support large systems of disparate digital twins.

I also worked as the Applications Engineer for the Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) Center at Clemson, where I trained students and faculty on a wide variety of engineering software tools as well as PLM processes such as Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE).
